## 1. Internal Screening & Idea Elimination Process

To ensure only rigorous, defensible, and realistic startup opportunities were shortlisted, a broad initial pool of concepts was evaluated against strict feasibility, regulatory, and commercial criteria. The following five concepts were formally eliminated during preliminary review:

| Concept Name | Category | Fatal Flaws Identified | Rejection Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Smart Drone for Bastar Forest Fire Detection** | Aerial Robotics | `Regulatory/Security Barrier, High Capex, Unmaintainable in Field` | Severe regulatory and security friction: Bastar is designated as a sensitive security corridor with stringent DGCA and local police airspace restrictions on UAV flights. Unrealistic student budget: Reliable long-endurance autonomous thermal drones cost >₹1.5 - ₹3 Lakhs, far exceeding student prototype feasibility. High operational vulnerability: Canopy density in Sal forests obscures thermal ground signatures; maintenance in rural Bastar lacks spare motor/esc supply chains. |
| **Blockchain-Enabled Mahua Supply Chain Traceability Platform** | Software / Web3 | `Pure Software Cliché, Zero Hardware Demonstrability, Severe User Disconnect` | No physical prototype or hardware defensibility: Fails the requirement of building and physically demonstrating a hardware/physical prototype. Gross mismatch with ground reality: Bastar forest gatherers lack smartphones, cellular connectivity, and digital literacy; smart contracts cannot verify whether Mahua was harvested hygienically. Solves a non-critical symptom: The real economic loss occurs due to ground contamination and fungal rot, not lack of blockchain hashes. |
| **Solar-Powered E-Rickshaw Retrofit Kit with Swappable Battery** | Electric Vehicles | `ARAI Homologation Barrier, High Liability & Capex, Incumbent Dominance` | Prohibitive regulatory certification: Automotive homologation under CMVR (ARAI / ICAT) costs ₹15-30 Lakhs and takes 12-18 months, impossible for an engineering student prototype. Capital intensity: Battery pack assembly and testing requires heavy capex, thermal testing chambers, and significant liability risk. Crowded market with heavily funded incumbents: Startups like Altigreen, Euler Motors, and local unorganized Chinese retrofitters already dominate price-sensitive Tier-2/3 auto markets. |
| **AI Voice Chatbot App for Tribal Tele-Medicine** | HealthTech Software | `Clinical Safety Risk, Pure Software App, Zero Diagnostic Moat` | Dangerous clinical hallucinations: Large language models fine-tuned on low-resource tribal dialects risk generating fatal diagnostic errors in acute conditions (snakebite, cerebral malaria). Lack of physical diagnostic capability: Advice without physical biological testing (turbidity, bilirubin, temperature, blood count) leaves doctors unable to prescribe treatments. Unmonetizable business model: Tribal patients cannot pay subscription fees, and state health departments do not procure uncertified diagnostic chatbots. |
| **Smart IoT Waste Bin with Automated Sensor Lid & GSM Overflow Alert** | Smart Cities Cliché | `Generic College Cliché, Sensor Fouling in Real Waste, Zero Business Viability` | Classic college hackathon cliché: One of the most overused, generic student projects; instantly rejected by serious venture investors and national judges. Zero municipal procurement traction: Municipalities (like Jagdalpur Nagar Nigam) suffer from lack of basic waste processing infrastructure, not bin-lid automation; ultrasonic sensors get covered in grease/dirt within 2 days. Negative unit economics: Adding ₹3,000 electronics to a ₹500 plastic bin creates vandalism and battery theft without generating revenue. |

## 2. Standardized Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) Scoring

All 20 candidate ventures were scored across 14 standardized dimensions on a 1.0 to 10.0 scale. The composite score is derived using a weighted linear combination reflecting hackathon-winning potential, ground feasibility, and enterprise sustainability.

### Weight Distribution Across Dimensions
- **Problem Severity**: 12% (Is the problem life-threatening or economically catastrophic?)
- **Prototype Feasibility**: 12% (Can a student team build a functioning physical prototype for <₹25,000?)
- **Existing Demand**: 10% (Are buyers actively seeking solutions or losing money?)
- **Student Team Feasibility**: 8% (Can B.Tech engineering students execute the skills?)
- **Market Scalability**: 8% (Can this scale from Bastar across Central India and nationally?)
- **Competitive Differentiation**: 8% (Does it have an insurmountable moat over existing alternatives?)
- **Revenue Potential**: 8% (Is the business model profitable and sustainable?)
- **Social/Environmental Impact**: 8% (Does it create measurable livelihood or ecological impact?)
- **Competition/Hackathon Potential**: 7% (Will this blow judges away in 5 minutes on stage?)
- **Local Pilot Feasibility**: 7% (Can the first pilot happen right here in Bastar/Jagdalpur?)
- **Grant/CSR Potential**: 5% (Can it tap into Bastar DMF, DST PRAYAS, BIRAC BIG, or NMDC CSR?)
- **Technical Defensibility**: 5% (Is there real engineering, or is it an easily copied app?)
- **Regulatory Simplicity**: 4% (Can it be deployed without years of red tape?)
- **Capital Efficiency**: 3% (Can the venture reach cash flow breakeven on modest seed capital?)
